## Architecture Decisions

### Content Permissions Model
- **Configurable per group** - Not one-size-fits-all
- **Default**: posts='everyone', events='admins'
- **Rationale**: Encourages engagement while preventing calendar chaos

### Pinned Posts as Announcements
- **No separate announcement system** - Reuse posts with `is_pinned` flag
- **Max 3 limit** - Prevents announcement fatigue
- **Admin-only** - Maintains content quality

### Google Places in Modals
- **Challenge**: Modal timing issues with JavaScript initialization
- **Solution**: Script runs when `$showModal` is true, with retry mechanism
- **Pattern**: Matches main event creation (CreateActivity.php)
