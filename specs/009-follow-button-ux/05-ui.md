## Choices (decided 2026-10-23: owner said "you decide")

1. **Placement:** A. Pill in the card header; the price moves into the chip row. Own events keep the price in the header and show no button.
2. **Posts:** no follow button on post cards (following only notifies about events). Revisit if posts ever notify.
3. **First-time pop-up:** stored per user (`users.follow_intro_seen_at`), so it follows them across devices.

## Deviations from the mockup

- A guest tapping Follow is sent to the login page; the mockup's login pop-up was not built (the feed requires login, so this only affects the event page and search).
- The success toast and failure toast use the app's existing toast component; its wording is "Following {name}. You'll be notified of their events."
- Unfollow confirm and the intro pop-up are one shared component, `x-fl.follow-modals`, used by the feed and the event page.

## New patterns proposed

- Named follow button ("Follow {name}") with a 44px touch target and a truncating label. Candidate for the pattern library at Freeze.
