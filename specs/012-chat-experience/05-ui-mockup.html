Chat experience — mockup

Spec 012 (placeholder number) · Status: APPROVED (delegated) · Synthetic data only · 390px phone first, then desktop. Interactive: tap a bubble for the action sheet, tap a quote or the pin to jump.

What this proposes (all decided under owner delegation, 2026-10-23): (1) The thread fills the screen and scrolls inside it; the composer stays pinned. Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a line. (2) Your message appears instantly (faded while sending, red with Retry if it fails); no blocking overlay. (3) Consecutive messages from one person group under one name and avatar; day separators; clock times. (4) "Load earlier" at the top; a "New messages" divider where you left off. (5) Tap a bubble: quick reactions (👍 means "I'm in"), Reply (to anyone, including yourself), Pin (host or admin only). (6) A pinned message sits at the top; tapping it jumps to the message. (7) Quotes jump to the original. Members-only sending shows a join bar. (8) The post page shows the last 3 messages and "Open chat" instead of the whole chat. (9) The dead bell button is removed.
